Lyvra privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

What Lyvra is

Lyvra is a media player for LG webOS TVs and Android phones and tablets. It plays playlists (M3U or Xtream Codes) that you add yourself. Lyvra does not provide, host or sell any channels, films or other content.

What stays on your device

Your playlists (including their addresses, usernames and passwords), settings, favorites, watch history, resume points, reminders and any keys you enter are stored only on your TV or phone. On Android they are excluded from Google cloud backup. Lyvra has no user accounts and no server that collects them. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

Connections Lyvra makes

  • Your playlist provider: to load channels, the TV guide and to play streams, using the details you entered.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database): to show film and series information (plot, cast, artwork, rating). Lyvra sends only the title and year of the film or series you open. No personal data is sent.
  • Optional services you turn on: OpenSubtitles (subtitle search, with your account), Trakt (watch progress, with your account), and free internet TV guides you add. Each of these receives only what it needs (for example a film title) under its own privacy policy.
  • Phone pairing, backup and restore: when you scan a QR code, details travel between your phone and TV through the public relay ntfy.sh. They are encrypted on the device with a key contained only in the QR code, so the relay sees scrambled data. The phone page is served from GitHub Pages. Backups are saved as a file on your phone, not by us. The Android app asks for no camera, location, contacts or storage permissions; it uses only internet access.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data clears history and Continue watching; removing a playlist deletes its data; uninstalling Lyvra removes everything it stored on the device.

Children

Lyvra is not directed at children. A PIN lock can hide adult categories.
